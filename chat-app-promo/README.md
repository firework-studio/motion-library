# Chat App Promo

A 43-second product film for a concept chat app where you make videos just by messaging. A line types itself onto black, bold words swap in one after another, and the app icon grows out of a phone's camera hole. A request gets answered with a finished film card, quick actions slide in, one tap sorts everything, the film ships, and a fire reaction drops onto the bubble. The camera pulls back over a rising planet, the chat shrinks into the app icon, a tagline types out, and a ring of light draws it into the Firework Studio mark. Music and sound effects land on every cut.
