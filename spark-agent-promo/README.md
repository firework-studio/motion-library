# Spark Agent Promo

A 65-second product film for Spark, a concept motion design agent, told as problem, turn, product, features and sign-off. Interface cards linked by orange dashed lines drift around a line of words that swap one by one, a full-width question slides across the frame, the mark resolves in place and its letters slide out of a blur, and chat bubbles pop from their avatars as the camera pushes in. Full-screen orange keywords divide the feature beats: a reference applied with its timecode running, flags on a timeline, a talking pill whose light and waveform follow the voice, comments fixed one by one, and a closing mark with its taglines. Narration, music and sound effects are mixed in.
