# Launch Film Machine

A 39-second launch film that shows how a launch film gets made, as an isometric machine on a bench. Two currents of stills and requests drift past a still headline; one still grows into a card where a one-line prompt is typed. The machine is drawn part by part, its lamp warms up in WebGL, a pointer drags the lamp fader and opens a drawer, a pick arm lays the reference card on a timeline belt that is read shot by shot with `power3.out`, and the projector plays the finished film onto the screen. The card returns to the wall, and a hand from Michelangelo's fresco points at the repository.
